### 2.1 Independent binding use

All decisions in this section remain **user-tentative**. The control layout below does not imply that the Scene consumer's workspace is already accounted. Its pre-reservation workspace and release/handoff contract must also close as specified below; none of the proposed controls is claimed to exist.

#### Current layout and accounting gap

The fixed independent route is `K/O/P/U = 0/0/0/1` plus distinct `W_u` and `D_u`, not another construction/replacement reservation (`docs\design\04-capacity-and-workload-profiles.md:182-189`). Process `UseCell` has eight four-byte fields in a declared 128-byte slot; the existing owner/coordinator roles do not issue independent use (`sources\TerraFX.Runtime\Entities\WorldGuidRegistry.cs:57-58`; `sources\TerraFX.Runtime\Entities\WorldGuidRegistry.cs:1879-1889`). A U slab therefore costs exactly `64 + 256*128 = 32832` bytes under the existing array charge, including spare capacity (`sources\TerraFX.Runtime\Entities\WorldGuidRegistry.cs:28`; `sources\TerraFX.Runtime\Entities\WorldGuidRegistry.cs:140-145`).

Domain `InterestEntry` is also 128 bytes, with generation/state/bytes/kind/root-index/root-generation fields; root entries are 256 bytes. The registrar has a fixed 4096-byte control charge (`sources\TerraFX.Runtime\Entities\WorldAdmissionDomain.cs:27`; `sources\TerraFX.Runtime\Entities\WorldAdmissionDomain.cs:2440-2482`). **Current operation credits are not interest cells:** reservation increments `_worldBytes` and `_activeOperationCreditCount`, then constructs a credit object; its count limit is 4096, distinct from the 65536 interest ceiling (`sources\TerraFX.Runtime\Entities\WorldAdmissionDomain.cs:19-24`; `sources\TerraFX.Runtime\Entities\WorldAdmissionDomain.cs:1168-1198`). The object also allocates a `Lock` and each retained use allocates `UseLease` (`sources\TerraFX.Runtime\Entities\WorldDomainOperationCredit.cs:10-16`; `sources\TerraFX.Runtime\Entities\WorldDomainOperationCredit.cs:39-55`). Thus calling the existing helpers does not by itself provide two named cells, a rooted failure anchor or a complete control-allocation charge.

#### Proposed fixed ownership shape

Use one separately allocated `WorldGuidUseControl`, with **256 bytes charged before allocation** under the selected x64 profile. Embed `WorldDomainRetentionAnchor` as a **128-byte value field**, not another object. There is no private `Lock`, separate `WorldHistoryInterest`/`WorldDomainOperationCredit` wrapper, per-use lease object or cleanup list for this route; the existing process/domain/binding gates protect transitions.

The control's byte grant is exactly **`B_op = 256 + W`**, where W is the admitted finite envelope for working/result/task/pin storage **not already charged** to existing Scene/asset reservations. It may conservatively reserve a peak rather than materialize that many bytes. For this actual Scene consumer W is **not zero**; its currently missing terms and proposed capped/incremental closure follow below. Charge B_op once to `_worldBytes`, store it in `D_u.Bytes`, and retain or transfer each funded obligation through actual cleanup/quarantine. A 256-byte metadata-only grant cannot fund Scene work. Domain admission requires `_registryCharge + _pendingGrowthBytes + _worldBytes + B_op <= MaximumBytes`; registry metadata separately remains within `min(134217728, MaximumBytes)`. Both constraints precede allocation (`sources\TerraFX.Runtime\Entities\WorldAdmissionDomain.cs:81-86`; `sources\TerraFX.Runtime\Entities\WorldAdmissionDomain.cs:1174-1194`; `docs\design\04-capacity-and-workload-profiles.md:113-117`).

Reserve two interest positions, not merely two counters. Current interest growth searches for only one free slot and grows all arrays with root capacity, capped at 4096 (`sources\TerraFX.Runtime\Entities\WorldAdmissionDomain.cs:1854-1873`; `sources\TerraFX.Runtime\Entities\WorldAdmissionDomain.cs:2010-2073`). The proposed paired route must prepare enough free nonretired entries and separately grow the **existing** interest array, up to its 65536 ceiling, without fabricating roots. Charge owned nonempty backing `A(n)=64+128*n` (shared empty backing has no owned-array charge): before growth old A(m) remains in `_registryCharge` and complete new A(n) enters `_pendingGrowthBytes`. On installation increase `_registryCharge` by `A(n)-A(m)` and retain **A(m)** as pending retirement charge until the old backing/readers actually release; only then subtract A(m). This preserves the full old+new coexistence peak, not merely the final size difference. Registry-charge recomputation must use the actual interest capacity, not infer it from root capacity as the current formula does (`sources\TerraFX.Runtime\Entities\WorldAdmissionDomain.cs:150-172`). All registry-array replacement must respect the single fixed growth ticket. Allocate outside registry/domain/binding gates; copy current entries and install under the domain gate after ticket/array-generation revalidation. Do not copy mutable old entries concurrently outside that gate. Release the ticket's old backing outside gates after readers quiesce. No reference to an array element escapes across growth.

The process U ceiling stays 131072; each domain additionally admits only available I positions and **one of its 4096 operation-count credits**. Quarantined/prospective uses count. None of those ceilings promises simultaneous byte fit or adds a lineage root/key/edge.

#### Scene consumer W: pre-reservation peak and transfer

**Current fact: the plan reservation is too late to fund counting.** Ordinary `PrepareAsync` loads the root Scene, constructs the builder, and calls `BuildAsync`; `BuildAsync` awaits the whole `CountAndMeasureAsync` traversal **before** `reserveStorage(requiredBytes)`, then allocates/expands the final plan (`sources\TerraFX.Runtime\Scenes\ScenePrefabExpansionPlanner.cs:103-141`; `sources\TerraFX.Runtime\Scenes\ScenePrefabExpansionPlanner.cs:653-709`). The returned estimate is calculated before final expanded arrays, not after the complete ordinary plan. Restore's separate measuring route additionally disposes a measuring builder, reserves plan workspace, and builds/counts again before resource measurement (`sources\TerraFX.Runtime\Scenes\ScenePersistenceRestorer.cs:95-140`). Sequential passes need the maximum of their actual peaks, not an invented simultaneous pair of complete plans.

Do not charge that same backing again as temporary cache metadata. It does **not** fund higher-owner Dictionary/List/builders, async frames, callback scratch or result/cleanup controls.

The missing term is **`W_missing = max_t(B_input/bootstrap + B_count/cache + B_async/result + B_uncovered_build + B_uncovered_callback + B_unreleased_cleanup)`**, counting only storage not already owned by a valid reservation at time t, including full old/new backing during resize and real overlaps. Code provides count/depth limits and partial array terms, but not a complete qualified capacity/task/callback/release inventory. `MaximumExpandedBytes` is checked only after counting (`sources\TerraFX.Runtime\Scenes\SceneExpansionLimits.cs:20-34`; `sources\TerraFX.Runtime\Scenes\ScenePrefabExpansionPlanner.cs:1199-1207`); it currently bounds the estimated output, not this prior peak. Therefore neither W=0 nor `W=256*N+2048` is an honest complete envelope for the existing consumer.

**Proposed minimal closure, including incremental Prefab funding:**

1. Add the missing preparation-peak/ownership rules to the Scenes contract, with the closed Rendering callback inventory and Assets' existing release obligations; no new source schema or public option is needed. Use the already selected `MaximumExpandedBytes` as a conservative **whole-preparation coexistence cap**, now explicitly covering temporary plus retained higher-owner storage. It becomes a valid bound only when every allocation/growth and compiler/task/wrapper envelope is admitted before execution. Until bootstrap/async footprints are qualified, safely prepay that cap in D_u rather than run the legacy planner with 256 bytes; this reserves capacity, not a cap-sized array. Smaller exact envelopes may follow qualification.
2. Split nonallocating input-shape/count validation from allocating freeze. Fund one preowned Scenes preparation context before either option copy, root Scene load, default builder construction or async invocation. Root that context through the existing anchor's opaque-owner reference. Its bounded fields retain the root/pending lease, builder, provisional plan/resource/result and release obligations through handoff; no new cleanup collection is allocated on failure. Bound suspended-frame/task/result footprints as part of this context's inventory, not with the unexplained 2048 allowance.
3. **Incremental D_u precharging before each new unique Prefab is the minimum cache repair, but is not the whole repair.** Cache hits need no new cache/lease admission. Before a miss calls `LoadFromPackageAsync`, admit the pending-lease/task envelope and complete Dictionary old+new capacity peak, then retain the returned lease in the context before validation/insertion. Only successful insertion transfers that pending obligation to the cache. Charge from explicit qualified capacities, not simply 256 per lookup; bootstrap, traversal/other growable buffers and resource callbacks have their own precharges. If bytes are not already prepaid, a closed private D_u growth transition must validate the same U/domain/root generations, captured finite cap, binding and joint byte limits before adding the delta to D_u/`_worldBytes`; it creates no new U/interest/count. The existing credit has readonly `_bytes`, so this transition is **proposed**, not an existing facility (`sources\TerraFX.Runtime\Entities\WorldDomainOperationCredit.cs:10-22`).
4. Preserve closure ordering: a fresh `TryReserveOperationCredit` after external closure is not permitted. Incremental allocation may consume the original prepaid cap; genuinely increasing charged bytes after closure requires the specifically admitted finite-growth authority above, not treating a maximum as an unspent byte reservation. The safe initial implementation prepays the cap. Capacity/stale-target refusal occurs before the next decode/callback/allocation, while prior consumers remain anchored until actual cleanup.
5. **Acquiring `WorldSceneStorageReservation` is an ownership-transfer point, not permission to release all W.** Identify C, the already funded allocations **or reserved capacity** that the new R actually covers and will own. Under exact binding/domain revalidation, admit R and transfer C atomically: `WorldData += R`, `D_u.Bytes -= C`, net domain charge `+= R-C`; validate remaining World/coexistence capacity before mutation. With the full preparation cap prepaid, take the receiver's R allowance from that cap (C=R when fully funded), preserving the remaining temporary/result allowance; do not charge the same reserved capacity twice. Do not release D_u first and hope R can then be acquired. The receiver must own the capacity before it allocates, and each allocation's ownership moves before its former owner ends; do not automatically take `C=256*N+2048`. Cache backing/leases following the plan may transfer, while temporary old buffers, builder/async/result state, callback scratch and unfinished cleanup remain under D_u. Separate measuring-pass workspace releases only after that pass's real consumers/disposal complete.
6. Keep the context and applicable R grants attached through resource preparation, result publication/activation transfer and last consumer cleanup. Replace cleanup-time builder allocations with preowned empty state and make release report/ownership preservation explicit: do not null/detach the only owning graph or surrender its reservation before remaining releases are known. Constructor, decode/insertion, cancellation, resource-validation and cleanup failures retain the exact remaining graph/credits in the preowned anchor. The existing detach/`finally` patterns alone do not establish that boundary.

#### Admission and rollback order

1. **Capture and preflight.** At the legal World boundary copy its exact lifetime, process grant and lineage/reservation facts. Validate request/cancellation without allocating preparation state and select the admitted W/cap before admission; do not determine W by running the unreserved counting/resource-measurement path. Allocating input freeze and async bootstrap follow funded U/context publication. Under process registry → exact domain → binding gates, require the same active lifetime and full `IsActive` predicate: exact K/O/owner-U generations, WorldGuid, owner-U role/state and K/O associations (`sources\TerraFX.Runtime\Entities\WorldGuidRegistry.cs:291-320`). Also validate the source domain reservation and committed nonretired lineage root, including root generation, WorldGuid, branch, namespace and current lineage (`sources\TerraFX.Runtime\Entities\WorldAdmissionDomain.cs:1525-1556`; `sources\TerraFX.Runtime\Entities\WorldAdmissionDomain.cs:2087-2101`). The constructor-oriented `WorldLifetimeControl.IsBoundTo` accepts prepared states, not active-use admission; do not use it as this predicate (`sources\TerraFX.Runtime\Entities\WorldLifetimeControl.cs:186-201`).
2. **Fund before constructing.** Recheck domain openness, byte/operation bounds and `active I + prospective I + 2 <= 65536`. Issue the fixed raw ticket, increment operation count once, reserve B_op and two prospective interest positions. This is only prospective accounting, not a usable `WorldGuidUse`. Release all gates, allocate the 256-byte control, initialize its invalid/unissued anchor, then transfer the raw grant into that anchor and link it at the domain's intrusive head. A single raw ticket serializes only this pre-control interval; transferred controls are bounded by the operation-count ceiling. If construction fails, finally returns that exact generation's raw bytes/count/prospective positions; no worker, root consumer or U exists yet.
3. **Prepare backing outside admission gates.** Through the fixed growth tickets prepare the domain pair and one U slot. Existing process slab growth already reserves a G ticket/bytes before allocating outside `_admissionGate` (`sources\TerraFX.Runtime\Entities\WorldGuidRegistry.cs:1307-1407`). Do not call allocating domain helpers while holding the process gate; current helpers keep `_growthGate` through construction (`sources\TerraFX.Runtime\Entities\WorldAdmissionDomain.cs:1172-1194`; `sources\TerraFX.Runtime\Entities\WorldAdmissionDomain.cs:1503-1523`). The proposed raw/ticket path releases **all** such scopes before allocation. Domain closure or binding revocation during preparation triggers rollback after running allocation joins; it cannot refund a returned/installed slab.
4. **Obtain `D_u`, then `W_u`.** Under the ordered gates, revalidate the exact binding/root/reservation and open domain again. Precompute all counter changes and require two free entries. Install `D_u` with its already prepaid B_op/control, then `W_u` with the pinned root; publish the pair or neither, convert the two prospective positions to active I, and increment history count once. Do not increment bytes/operation count a second time. Allocation or `WorldHistoryInterest` construction is unnecessary: retain the existing slot/generation protocol inline (`sources\TerraFX.Runtime\Entities\WorldAdmissionDomain.cs:1572-1589`; `sources\TerraFX.Runtime\Entities\WorldHistoryInterest.cs:59-77`).
5. **Obtain independent U and publish authority.** Reenter the same ordered gates after any capacity preparation, repeat binding/root checks, and validate both issued domain entries against this control/generation. If no U remains, leave gates to prepare backing or refuse; never allocate under them. Install one U with exact K/O association/control reference, increment the key's retained-role count and the binding's independent-use count, and publish the complete issued carrier/control state atomically. Store the independent count in BindingCell padding; its current payload uses only 20 of 128 bytes (`sources\TerraFX.Runtime\Entities\WorldGuidRegistry.cs:1821-1829`). Add no O/P/K. An earlier `IsActive` result alone is insufficient after gates were dropped. Only after publication may asynchronous Scene work escape.
6. **Rollback in reverse, with no usable output.** Before publication, unwind any reserved U, then `W_u`, then `D_u`, its operation bytes/count and remaining prospective/raw grant. Validate all exact generations/roles and precompute changes before the allocation-free rollback; cleanup uses its own issued authority even if the domain has closed or the target revoked. `W_u` release uses the current root/history-count protocol, including owner-retired pruning, rather than GUID-based removal (`sources\TerraFX.Runtime\Entities\WorldAdmissionDomain.cs:249-332`). Operation release subtracts B_op/count exactly once (`sources\TerraFX.Runtime\Entities\WorldAdmissionDomain.cs:334-363`). Unlink/clear the empty control only after actual allocation/storage cleanup. Keep installed process slabs charged at high water; keep installed domain backing charged until replacement/retirement. Task completion and physical release run after leaving every gate.

#### Pre-anchored failure, handoff and actual release

Link the charged anchor **before** any fallible Scene callback, asset/work allocation or asynchronous escape; retain it through prepared-result ownership. Admission therefore needs no allocation when cleanup fails: change only this control/anchor to quarantined, retain U/`W_u`/`D_u`/B_op and actual unreleased resources, and record bounded numeric failure facts. The existing process U reference roots the control → domain → intrusive node chain even after public wrappers disappear; no new quarantine collection or cleanup-time node is created.

Revocation closes acquisition immediately; captured finite work may finish safe consumption/cleanup but cannot apply to a successor with equal GUID. Closure before final publication refuses; closure after publication does not revoke the accepted prepaid grant. The binding owner retains O/owner-U/`C_o`, not the independent credits.
