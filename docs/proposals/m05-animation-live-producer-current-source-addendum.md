# M05 Animation live-producer current-source addendum

## 1. Disposition and bounded outcome

**Tentative implementation recommendation under the user's unblocker authorization; not a maintained-contract amendment, implementation result, or qualification acceptance.** Complete the native-authored package → Scene → pre-registered World → live Animation/root-request → committed Headless extraction → Standard Branch restore path. The blocker is its missing owner integration, not another missing playback catalogue or resource codec.

This corrects the current-source findings in sections 2 and 6 of [the pre-construction proposal](m05-animation-preconstruction-producer-boundary.md), whose older freeze must not be reused. It also makes the implementation observations in [aggregate attachment closure](m05-animation-aggregate-attachment-closure.md) historical, without replacing that proposal's selected contract. Preserve:

- [Rectification](m05-owner-contract-closure-rectification.md) and [Seek addendum](m05-owner-contract-closure-rectification-addendum.md): selected declarations, canonical state, commands, timing, events and versioned rendering.
- [Runtime ports](m05-owner-contract-closure-runtime-ports.md) and [typed restore-source addendum](m05-owner-contract-closure-runtime-ports-addendum.md): exact lower capabilities, captured families, ownership and restore joins.
- [Projected-membership addendum](m05-animation-aggregate-attachment-projected-membership-addendum.md): replacement preparation before reference preflight and Structural installation.
- [Root-resource closure](m05-root-motion-resource-record-array-closure.md) and [rigid-composition rectification](m05-root-motion-rigid-composition-rectification.md): existing request schema, historical joins, transport and numerical decisions.

The original [owner-closure proposal](m05-owner-contract-closure.md) remains motivation, not an up-to-date absence inventory. Chapter 20 owns behavior; Chapters 10/27 own encoded state/persistence, Chapter 30 owns public declarations, Chapter 29 owns dependency direction, and Chapter 35 owns prerequisites and acceptance. Tentative permission does not establish their formal adoption or evidence acceptance.

## 5. Fail-closed rule and unresolved gates

**Scene preparation remains fail-closed.** Today's normal composition rejects an unregistered player component in `SceneLoader.ValidateComponentRegistrations`, and `RenderSceneAssetBundle.FillAssetIds` refuses non-BindPose input on its selected static path. Adding metadata, the player registration or an Animation feature bit must not turn either refusal into successful unsupported preparation. A selected Scene cannot become ready until its complete required Animation assets, target map, measured ownership and activation transaction exist. No component skipping, late registration, stage-7 I/O repair, implicit Clip Scene activation or renderer-side shadow clock is permitted.

Remaining gates are:

- **Chapter 35 Animation registration/state/extraction:** the concrete support/phase/Scene/member/cohort graph and its full reservation/lifetime proof are missing. The existing tentative declarations guide implementation; formal owner-contract adoption, including the selected Chapter 30 surface and exact callable closure, remains open. Any departure or genuinely undefined operand stays an explicitly owned contract gap.
- **Selected M03-B persistence:** active-player preparation, Animation control dispatch and the post-restore derived join are missing; the source caches, historical-only request joins and fresh comparison are already present and must be preserved.
- **World-bound/native/consumer qualification:** actual live producer observations have not run here. Retry the nonzero GPU witness only after a real animated snapshot, funded diagnostic path and actual backend are available. Public-only consumers, NativeAOT, target variants, measured peaks and full milestone acceptance remain separate.
- **Other owners:** required Scene preparation/receiver/cleanup guarantees retain their M03-B owners; collision/feedback retains M06/M07; existing M08/M09 physical-input/native-failure/timeout deferrals are unchanged. Do not repeat unchanged blocked probes or add synthetic substitutes to close them.

## 6. Scope of this update

The committed reader-only change replaces nested `ImmutableArray` builders plus author-constructor copies with exact arrays adopted by internal reader-owned factories. Ordinary author constructors still copy. It also removes the now-unneeded duplicate-backing multiplier from the existing typed-read reservation function. The Animation Clip binder additionally accepts a retained Scene plan, rejects a different package backing or Scene id, and validates against the supplied expanded-key map without preparing a second Scene. These changes do not add a World registration token, participant, phase actor, player producer, Scene activation or animated extraction path. They change no maintained design/roadmap contract, schema bytes or protected fixture. The five unrelated dirty Scene/Sprite tests remain outside this change.
