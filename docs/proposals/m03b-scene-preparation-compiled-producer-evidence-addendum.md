- There is no allocation-profiler, native-code, NativeAOT, numerical J/K, full-preclaim or last-access result here. Current IL is not the final candidate's layout or evidence for Release or another async implementation.

## 2. Narrow production mechanism to make concrete

Use the handoff's existing `PreparedScene` carrier and explicit, asynchronous-continuation `TaskCompletionSource<PreparedScene>`; do not introduce a second operation owner.

1. **Fund entry before construction.** Preserve nonallocating shape/scalar validation and pre-cancellation ordering. Acquire exactly the preparation use for captured P. Through its existing issuer, preclaim the complete coexisting entry envelope in section 3 using nonallocating scalar accounting, before constructing the root or any closure/delegate/Task/TCS or invoking an async helper. A valid P below this reached requirement refuses synchronously, with no asset work or smaller-cap retry. Do not allocate a capturing funding callback in order to request its own grant.
2. **Construct and anchor the hidden result.** Change the private constructor to accept the validated root AssetId without a plan. Initialize bounded root/Lock/ledger state, then immediately install that exact object with `use.Control.SetRetainedOwner(root)`, before freeze or producer work. Initialize the prepaid promise and bound callbacks under this root's ownership. Until installation succeeds, the synchronous entry guard alone owns the unescaped use and successful claims; afterward only the root unwinds them. Constructor field initializers are part of the preclaim, not work that can be charged inside the constructor afterward.
3. **Retain the complete operation, not its newest leaf.** Freeze synchronously and commit the snapshot/backing obligation to the root before relinquishing entry aliases. Keep root links to the in-flight loader/producer state, current builder or plan, pending child Task, and staged partial receiver/lease/cleanup debt. Route ordinary planner retention and quarantine through the handoff's root-owned phase/pending slots; never replace the use's root with the loader, a lease, builder, plan or activation request. Stage receiving/partial ownership before fallible acquisition or attachment, and clear the previous slot only after an all-or-none transfer. A pending Task reference alone is not proof that every partial resource is rooted; establish the actual compiled strong-reference path.
4. **Replace only ordinary outer orchestration.** Make the handoff's thin non-`async` Start, plan-ready and resource-ready driver concrete around the real planner, existing validation and real resource preparer. Precreate its two bound callbacks; keep its pending slots and three ordinary invocation parts in the root. Reserve a part before inline invocation or registration can expose its callback. Registration may race or reenter; the registering part remains live through registration's return. Clear body/snapshot/plan locals before enabling the next stage. No `Task.Run`, `async void`, orphan `ContinueWith` Task, replacement worker or generic scheduler is proposed.
5. **Publish through an explicit ordinary boundary.** Capture the public Task in entry before starting the driver, and return that local without rereading a possibly drained root. Once real body consumers have ended or transferred, install the completed body and perform exactly one selected `TrySetResult(root)`, `TrySetException` or `TrySetCanceled` outside all gates. Use `RunContinuationsAsynchronously`; preserve ExecutionContext flow/restoration and existing lack of caller SynchronizationContext capture. Do not substitute `UnsafeOnCompleted` without proving the same semantics. Publication's actual return, all ordinary parts' ends and transitive producer ends are separate facts.

The final candidate must retain every remaining Scene-owned supplier/wrapper provision until its own qualified end or transfer. Removing the public async method exposes a useful source boundary; it does **not** make its still-async planner or resource supplier quiescent.

## 4. Producer, body and publication join

| Condition | Required meaning; not a substitute signal |
|---|---|
| **Body input boundary** | Before terminal publication, valid planning/resource/option consumers have ended or transferred to installed ownership. Available body cleanup may race immediately with publication; Admitted transfers the one body role and snapshot obligation to the exact request. Callback revocation alone is insufficient until callback/executor stack borrows unwind and expired context snapshot aliases are severed. |
| **Publication access ended** | The one selected `TrySet*` has actually returned; owned promise/outcome/exception-array references and the funded tail are handled without further public-promise or body access. A terminal Task, source `finally`, child `await`, `SetResult` call-site hit or `RunContinuationsAsynchronously` is not this condition. |
| **Ordinary parts ended** | Start, plan-ready and resource-ready each end once after their last owned access, including registration return. A reserved but not yet invoked callback is still a part. Uncertain registration failure is not proof it can be abandoned/refunded. |
| **Transitive producer end** | Each supplier's lowered publication/frame and Scene-created registration/dispatch/context wrapper has a production-valid last-access/transfer edge. The ordinary part counter proves none of these by itself. Any work after a proposed signal remains funded until its own end. |
| **Body ended** | Available disposal completed actual cleanup, or the exact admitted request reports final preparation-funded consumption/cleanup or installed transfer. Queue refusal captures nothing and ends no preparation claim. Quarantined body storage is retained ownership, not successful body completion. |
| **Final use release** | Publication/ordinary/transitive conditions and Body ended all hold; preparation claims have actually ended or transferred; the final tail itself has a qualified no-further-access disposition. The root elects one releaser and keeps itself installed through fallible lower release. Never call `ClearRetainedOwner(root)` first or let a copied use grant another release right. |

The admitted activation request reports body completion through the exact root backlink; it does **not** dispose an alias of the preparation use or independently refund the root's backing ledger. Its separately acquired activation use remains independent: **`W_a = P`**, admission `256 + P`, with its own request/completion provision held through its own `TrySet*` return. Do not fund the preparation tail from activation, shorten that tail, or treat existing activation constants as a new qualification result.

On claim-return or lower-release failure, preserve the full rooted still-owned graph and exact remaining grant/debt facts. Current lower release can release the process cell before domain release fails; do not report an atomic drain, recreate an ended cell or refund remaining interests. Use-only quarantine is for actual stopped-but-unreleased failures, not ordinary unknown epilogues or a substitute for joining still-consuming work.

**Distinguishing witness:** a caller-owned scheduler holds successful preparation dispatch after the Task becomes terminal. Another thread retrieves the result and disposes it, or admits and completes activation. The body/backing may end or transfer, but preparation producer/shared/publication provision remains charged while the publisher or another registration/supplier part still runs. Only the qualified joined last access permits its refund, once. This is a proposed observation, not an executed preparation race.

Retain the handoff's tentative **external-continuation provenance rider**: external registrations own their continuation/list/context/scheduler work and runtime adapters/queue capacity created solely to dispatch them, even inside `TrySet*`. External observer-requested metadata follows its observer. This is a proposed ownership policy, not an effect of TCS options and not a blanket CLR exemption. Scene-created registrations, context wrappers, Task publication bookkeeping, diagnostics and cleanup remain charged. External dispatch may still hold the Scene producer inside `TrySet*`. Without the rider or a separately reviewed runtime funding boundary, unrestricted observer fanout prevents an all-dispatch constant; increasing K cannot fix it.
