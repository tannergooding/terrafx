## 1. Scope, coordinates, and numerical profile

The engine implements its own rigid-body physics; references inform algorithms, not an external physics runtime. `TerraFX.Physics` depends only on `TerraFX.Core` and the base class library (BCL), as required by [29](29-project-setup-build-and-deployment.md)'s assembly DAG. `PhysicsWorld2D` and `PhysicsWorld3D` are standalone owner-bound services with their own simulation storage, explicit rate/origin and caller-provided owner-thread context; neither requires `TerraFX.Entities`, `TerraFX.Scenes`, `TerraFX.Hosting`, or an entity-component-system (ECS) `World`. They share lifecycle, filtering, events, constraints, and transactional concepts, not a falsely dimension-independent narrow phase. `Cross`-dimensional bodies never collide. `TerraFX.Integration`, a namespace in `TerraFX.Runtime` rather than a separate assembly/package, optionally composes these same services into the [13](13-jobs-and-frame-pipeline.md) pipeline; it does not implement a second solver. Physics has no Runtime dependency and still requires only the Core library package.

`Physics` uses frozen engine distance units, kilograms, seconds, and radians in chapter [03](03-shared-conventions-and-clocks.md)'s left-handed +X-right/ +Y-up/ +Z-forward world. Angular values follow its unchanged BCL/axial convention; 2D is XY with positive +Z angle counterclockwise from +X toward +Y. Default physical gravity is `(0, -9.81)` / `(0, -9.81, 0)` meters per second squared, converted once into the frozen unit profile. Fixed outer step dt = 1/`TickRate`, default 60 Hz, not derived from rounded `TimeSpan`. `Physics` does not promise cross-platform bitwise determinism. The binary32/compensated spatial profile plus the `LeftHandedYUpForwardZ1` coordinate profile, stable order/content/runtime/configuration are necessary but not sufficient for replay certification.

Public geometry uses the `TerraFX.Core`-owned `TerraFX.Numerics` types in [Chapter 06](06-owned-geometry-and-numerics-interchange.md), retaining standalone `Physics -> Core` plus BCL dependencies. Internal ordinary vectors/quaternions/inertia/solver arithmetic are binary32. Positions retain int32 cells and private compensated high/low offsets; uncertain geometric classifications use bounded exact dyadic/rational fallback under [08](08-binary32-kernels-and-predicates.md), not a required FP64 path. Do not round-trip simulation state through public floats each substep. Convert when accepting input and publishing output; never feed an extracted/interpolated render pose back into physics. Those geometry interchange pairs are exact binary32 component interchange, not conversion to solver precision or an admission bypass. Geometry profile `EngineGeometryBinary32Rows2` joins recorded execution identity independently of `LeftHandedYUpForwardZ1`.

`PrimitiveShapeGeometryContext2D/3D` under [23 section 1.2](23-custom-physics-shapes-queries-and-ccd.md#12-inert-primitive-validation-and-statistics) is a distinct geometry-only context for inert M03 primitive validation. It does not replace or truncate `PhysicsNumericalProfile2D/3D`; those internally constructed types retain the complete numerical/material profile in [30](30-public-api-review-surface.md). Geometry `Valid` proves neither full-profile spacing/default eligibility nor body/material/mass/inertia/capacity/workload admission. M06 derives the same primitive context from admitted full-profile settings and invokes the same validator; it adds its own owner/cooking/body admissions without changing the geometry outcome or statistics.

The calculation-frame bound follows [07](07-spatial-coordinates-and-indexing.md), not a limit on total world size. In the closed meter interval `[-4096, +4096]`, the largest interior binary32 gap is 0.000244140625 meters; the larger outward step is outside that interval. Validate physical local/frame spacing against `LinearSlop / 8`. Predicates use nearby/recentered frames, certified float filters and bounded exact fallback. Shape/mass ratios, solver counts, sleep/slop, and resource ceilings are chosen version-one tuning limits, not constants attributed to Catto or experimentally certified here. Rate/substep changes require separate [33](33-physics-acceptance-and-reference.md) workload qualification; a permitted configuration is not a promise that every arbitrarily stiff scene converges.

With kilometer units and 4096-unit chunks, a primitive geometry context can validate local geometry while the full Physics profile refuses fixed-spacing admission: the physical interior gap is `0.244140625 m`, exceeding `LinearSlop / 8 = 0.000625 m`. Do not enlarge fixed tolerances or infer full-profile eligibility from a geometry response. The geometry context's positive extent limits are representable for all Core unit exponents; scaled measure publication likewise does not impose a finite-single-float area/volume requirement.

## 2. Body ownership and optional integration

Each body stores its own center of mass (COM) position, rotation, COM linear velocity, world angular velocity, mass/inverse mass, local COM, local inertia/inverse inertia, accumulated force/torque, colliders, joint links, awake timer, motion mode, and stable handles. Static/kinematic bodies have zero inverse mass/inertia; static velocity is zero. Kinematic velocity is prescribed from a target, not collision response. A dynamic body responds to gravity/forces/constraints. Disabled bodies do not integrate, collide, trigger, or appear in queries; their identity and stored state remain valid. Sleeping is not disabling.

Static/kinematic mass records conventionally have mass/inertia 0 and `localCenter` 0, so their simulation center equals body origin; density does not fabricate finite mass for them. Explicit mass is dynamic-only. `RotationLocked` dynamics retain physical mass/inertia records but use zero inverse inertia. `Physics` checks only its dimensional owner/generation handles. Its optional association is `Core` `BindingId(Guid Value)`, with `Guid.Empty` meaning unbound; the value is opaque and immutable for a body's lifetime. `Physics` neither resolves it nor imposes ECS identity/uniqueness rules. `TerraFX.Integration` owns the `BindingId`-to-`Entity`-to-Body map and entity-owner checks.

`BodyPose` is the authoring/body-origin pose. `CenterOfMass` and `LinearVelocity` refer to the COM: `c = p + R * localCenter`, `p = c - R * localCenter`, and velocity of a world point `x` is `v + omega cross (x - c)`. In 2D `omega cross (x,y) = (-omega * y,omega * x)`. `Physics` has no render Z or scene hierarchy.

Collider/body poses accepted by standalone physics are rigid and contain no scale/shear fields. Authoring can explicitly bake scale into a *new* validated shape before admission, but cannot modify its runtime pose. Accepted 3D quaternion inputs must be finite with norm within `1e-5` of 1 and are normalized by the scaled binary32 kernel; zero/non-unit values outside that allowance are rejected. 2D input angles are finite radians reduced to their principal equivalent before trigonometry; the solver keeps the principal angle plus a checked long revolution count for continuous joint angles.

The optional `TerraFX.Integration` adapter enforces one body per entity and a scene-root binding with unit scale, no shear/reflection and rigid rotation. A 2D bound root rotates only about +Z; `Integration` preserves its non-simulated render Z. Non-root/scaled/sheared/non-finite bindings or X/Y rotations of a 2D root, including later hierarchy edits, reject the encompassing binding/structural request as `Integration` `UnsupportedPhysicsTransform`. No ECS query, scene decomposition or binding validator runs inside `TerraFX.Physics`. Visual children may have arbitrary visual transforms without adding implicit collision geometry.

Dynamic pose belongs to physics. `Integration` refuses ordinary ECS pose writes on bound roots; editor/scene adapters use `Teleport` for relocation or `SetKinematicTarget` for prescribed continuous motion. `Integration` consumes animation/root-motion records and emits typed kinematic targets or app-authored dynamic forces/impulses; `Physics` has no animation or controller dependency. At stage 5 `Integration` copies locally solved body-origin poses to ECS and resolves descendants. External render interpolation reads only encompassing-world snapshots published at stage 9.

| Optional [13](13-jobs-and-frame-pipeline.md) pipeline stage | Ownership and visibility |
|---|---|
| 0 `Begin` | `Integration` admits accepted external requests and prior external structural transactions; no integration yet |
| 1 `Input` / `Early` | Systems enqueue copied forces/impulses/requests with actual stage/system/partition keys |
| 2 `Animation` / Targets | `Integration` converts typed root-motion records; last ordered target wins |
| 3 `Transforms` | `Integration` validates roots/ownership and resolved hierarchy; `Physics` retains no component refs |
| 4 `Physics` | `Physics` preflights/stages/solves, then locally commits complete body/contact state and returns immutable raw output to `Integration` |
| 5 Pose Sync | `Integration` copies local body poses to ECS and resolves descendants |
| 6 Events / `Late` | `Integration` enriches sorted raw events for tick-local `Late` systems only; no external publication or callbacks during solving |
| 7 `Structural` | Join all queries/jobs; `Integration` preflights ECS/binding reservations and `Physics` structural/rebase plans before committing; resolve newly dirty transforms |
| 8 `Extract` | Build immutable render/audio/animation outputs; still no external world publication |
| 9 `Commit` | Only after stages 0 through 8, inclusive, succeed, publish encompassing-world completion/events/receipts/snapshots and advance its `TickIndex` |

Creating an entity/body at stage 7 of tick N makes its proxy visible to tick-local queries after that barrier and eligible for physics at N + 1, not retroactively at N. Destroying there removes its proxy immediately. `Physics` exposes prepare/commit structural plans independent of ECS; `Integration` coordinates their reservations with ECS and the binding map. Preflight rejection commits neither participant. This is a reservation/commit contract, not automatic rollback of earlier world stages or a guarantee that a later fault undoes a valid local commit.

Standalone callers use the same owner-thread services directly: enqueue definitions, `PrepareStructural`/`CommitStructural` at an idle boundary, then `Step(tick)` with the configured fixed rate. Before the first step, initialization creates bodies eligible for that first step; afterward boundary creations first simulate the next step. `Step` immediately makes its complete local output available to the standalone caller. There is no mandatory ECS scheduler, scene, extraction stage or host; the caller controls its idle boundaries.

## 3. Commands, lifecycle, and events

Simulation mutation is command-based, owner-thread applied at the stated barriers. `PhysicsCommandKey(byte Stage, int SystemOrder, int Partition, long Sequence)` has exactly chapter [13](13-jobs-and-frame-pipeline.md)'s lexicographic stage/registered-system-order/stable-partition/local-sequence ordering; reuse an identical `Core` key if shared. `Integration` supplies the actual emitting stage, explicit registered system order and stable partition ordinal, never hash/discovery/race-derived producer IDs. Standalone callers use `Stage` 0/`SystemOrder` = their registered stable producer ID/Partition 0 with local Sequence. Reject duplicate full keys within an admission tick, unknown stage values outside integer stage IDs 0 through 9 inclusive, sequence/count overflow and invalid inputs; concurrent arrival order is not authority.

The admission tick is separate from the order key. External requests arriving after `Begin`, and nonstructural requests emitted after stage 4 including `Late` stage 6, admit next tick and retain their original key; do not relabel `Late` work as `Stage` 0. Thus next tick's current `Early` `Stage` 1 work precedes admitted prior `Late` `Stage` 6 work. `Stage` identifies emission order, not execution phase: both execute in that next tick's stage 4 input batch, never a second `Late` solve. `Structural` gameplay requests wait for stage 7. Standalone `Step` freezes its accepted input journal at entry; later arrivals wait for its next `Step`. Shape cooking is an owner-thread asset operation outside step/query/event callbacks and publishes only a validated immutable asset, not a proxy.

Force/torque commands add, with checked finite results; force-at-point adds torque `(point-c) cross force`. Impulses immediately change *staged* `v`/`omega` using inverse mass/inertia. `SetVelocity` replaces velocities at its sequence position. Commands requesting dynamic operations on a non-dynamic body are refused. Force accumulators clear only on successful outer commit; they apply once per elapsed microstep, not once per solver iteration or CCD impact. Default gravity scale is 1 and damping is 0.

Force/torque/impulse/Wake/Sleep on disabled bodies are refused, not accepted then discarded without integration; `Teleport`/`SetVelocity` can edit their stored state. Damping, gravity scale, sleep permission, `RotationLocked` and `MotionMode` are frozen per-body creation fields in version one; changing them requires explicit body recreation, with documented contact ends. Mass/type replacements preserve body identity; collider/joint replacement invalidates the old handle and returns a new-generation handle. Replaced collider episodes end `ColliderRemoved`; type changes end Filtered only where pair eligibility is lost.

Kinematic target is a rigid body-origin pose for the outer tick. Derive COM velocity `(cTarget-cStart)/dt`, and shortest signed 2D angle difference / shortest quaternion axis-angle divided by dt. Interpolate uniformly during the tick. No target means zero velocity and unchanged pose, not continued previous target motion. Exactly pi chooses the positive 2D angle / lexicographically positive 3D axis. A kinematic target that exceeds bounds is refused before solving; targets are not teleports. Moving kinematics wake affected dynamics.

`Teleport` is a distinct operation with `VelocityPolicy.Preserve` or `Clear`, default Clear. It resets sleep time, invalidates warm starts, and ends old pair episodes with `Teleported`; new overlaps begin from the new location this tick. It is not a sweep. Enable/disable and filter changes applied to the staged physics step wake incident dynamic islands and invalidate affected pairs. Their raw events exist only after valid local commit; integrated external publication additionally requires stage 9. Body type, geometry, mass properties, and joint edits are structural, immutable replacements at an idle structural boundary.

Computed mass sums all non-sensor convex colliders with their density and rigid local transform. Surfaces have no mass and are static-only. A dynamic body with no massive collider requires explicit positive mass properties; do not assign a convenient mass of one. Explicit mass provides mass, local COM, and valid symmetric positive-definite inertia (or zero inverse inertia for `RotationLocked`). Sensors still have a geometric center/bounds but do not contribute mass. `Structural` mass edits preserve origin pose and origin-point velocity: `vNew = vOld + omega cross (cNew - cOld)`; this is not a momentum-conserving physical mass transfer. Clear warm starts and wake afterward.

Automatic joint breaking, arbitrary body-type changes during a solve, contact-modification callbacks, and user-provided per-pair collision callbacks are excluded. Deterministic material/filter data replaces callbacks.

Events describe collider-pair episodes, aggregating their mesh/child manifolds; distinct collider handles on the same pair of bodies remain distinct episodes. A solver candidate is not necessarily a Touching pair. `Begin` when minimum separation <= `TouchTolerance`; an existing episode ends only when no candidate has separation <= `EndTolerance`. Sensors use the same geometric tolerances but their manifolds create no impulse or island edge; body joints remain independent of sensor colliders. Stay is one optional summary at tick end, not one callback per iteration.

| Event / reason | Required observation |
|---|---|
| `BeginContact` / `BeginTrigger` | Pair, copied opaque `BindingIdA`/B, contact/sensor kind, timestamp, witnesses; one per new episode |
| `StayContact` / `StayTrigger` | At most one per surviving episode per outer tick; option frozen, default off |
| `EndContact` / `EndTrigger` | Pair and last immutable witnesses; `Separated`, `Filtered`, `Disabled`, `Teleported`, `ColliderRemoved`, `BodyDestroyed`, `Unbound`, or `WorldDisposed` |
| `BodySleep` / `BodyWake` | Body identity, cause, committed tick; at most one final sleep-state transition per tick |
| `PhysicsFault` | Fault code, phase, tick, optional body/pair/joint, counts/residuals; no contact/sleep batch for failed tick |

Observe transitions at microstep start/end and each CCD impact boundary; a `Begin` then End inside one tick is preserved. Sort events by `(PublicationTick, BoundaryKind, TickFraction, ColliderPairKey, EpisodeSequence, EventRank, BodyHandle)`, where `Begin` = 0/Stay = 1/End = 2/Sleep = 3/Wake = 4; boundary kind `FinalPrevious` = 0/Simulation = 1/Closure = 2. Body-only events use the all-zero pair key. Observed fractions use binary32 in the closed interval `[0, 1]`, not rounded `TimeSpan`. Same-fraction episode sequence preserves End-before-rebegin after a teleport/filter replacement. Pair keys compare `(OwnerId,Index,Generation)` lexicographically and put the lesser collider first; its normal points from A to B. `EpisodeSequence` is checked nonnegative `long` per pair; exhaustion faults instead of wrapping.

Final-barrier removal after `Late` cannot notify gameplay retroactively. Queue immutable raw End tombstones for the next successfully solved physics tick's `FinalPrevious` boundary, with `ObservedTick = N`, `TickFraction = 1`, `PublicationTick = N + 1`; new episodes follow them. `PublicationTick` is the output batch's intended tick, not proof of external world publication. Raw tombstones contain stale physics handles, copied `BindingIdA`/B and last witnesses, never `Entity` values. Standalone shutdown drains pending ends and remaining episodes into a caller-owned immutable closure batch before freeing storage even if no further `Step` occurs.

`TerraFX.Integration` owns entity enrichment and historical `Entity` tombstones. It captures mapping values before stage 7 removal and wraps the raw event as `PhysicsEntityEvent2D/3D(PhysicsEvent2D/3D Physics, Entity? EntityA, Entity? EntityB)` in `TerraFX.Integration`, not `TerraFX.Physics`. These are optional copied historical values, never live refs. `Entity`/binding loss causes the adapter to enqueue `DestroyBody` with explicit Unbound or `BodyDestroyed` reason; `Physics` does not observe entity storage or infer loss from an opaque `Guid`. Integrated closure enrichment/delivery belongs to the encompassing shutdown/fault policy, outside solving. `Integration` installs its replacement episode ledger at chapter [13](13-jobs-and-frame-pipeline.md)'s irrevocable stage 9 commit marker, before any external subscriber runs. Subscriber failure cannot retract that commit or the ledger's `Begin`/End transitions; it yields an explicit delivery failure while other subscribers continue. After a later pre-commit world fault, terminal external ends use that ledger's historical witnesses/bindings, not the newer local physics episode set. Never externally end an unpublished `Begin` or lose a committed active episode merely because its local End belonged to the failed tick.

The `PhysicsEntityEvent2D/3D` wrapper and optional historical `Entity?` values are transient Integration observations only. They are not portable reliable Event, Effect, EpisodeState, Begin, End or Terminal payload fields and are not serialized or hashed into canonical reliable state. Portable reliable registrations use descriptor-declared stable/historical references and witness data; they never persist `OwnerId`, `Index` or `Generation`, or fabricate a live handle during restore. Reject direct or nested `Entity`/nullable-`Entity` leaves in those portable payloads; `unmanaged` alone is not sufficient. The transient wrapper grants no live authority.

## 4. Preflight, solve, commit, fault

There are two locally committed slots (current/previous physics snapshots) and exclusive mutable staging. Include poses, velocities, forces, kinematic progress, enabled/filter values, mass state, proxy bounds/tree edits, contact feature caches and impulses, island/sleep state, event episodes/tombstones, statistics, and command-consumption cursor in the transaction. Shared immutable shape assets are pinned. No query, event callback or adapter observes incomplete staging. Locally committed physics output is distinct from the encompassing-world committed checkpoint/snapshot.

1. Validate tick/rate/origin version, physics handles, shapes, commands and known motion/range/inertia constraints; reserve command, proxy, pair, manifold, event and worst permitted working memory. Insufficient reservation faults before integration. `Integration` has already validated scene bindings at stage 3.
2. Clone/copy-on-write committed physics state into staging; apply this tick's ordered commands and wake propagation; derive kinematic motion.
3. For each of Substeps microsteps: integrate forces/damping, update bounds, collect/filter pairs, build manifolds/islands, warm-start once, solve velocity rows, run 13 CCD temporal segments, integrate poses, and solve position rows. Rebuild contacts at segment boundaries, and after final position correction for events.
4. Validate all resulting finite values, coordinate/motion limits, quaternion normalization, counts, reference integrity, and CCD certificates. Compute diagnostic residuals; a fixed-iteration residual above ordinary slop is not silently declared convergence. Check `NextTick` increment and construct/reserve the complete immutable output before changing any committed slot or cursor.
5. Atomically swap staging into locally committed physics state, clear consumed forces/commands and return the already prepared immutable `PhysicsStepOutput` with poses/raw event batch/results/statistics. This final swap allocates nothing and invokes no callback. No callback runs between microsteps or inside solving. Standalone `Step` publishes this complete local output immediately to its caller; integrated stage 4 only returns tick-local output to its adapter.

A numerically invalid solve or unproven CCD advance aborts *the entire outer physics step*. The stable physics fault codes are `CapacityExceeded`, `NumericOverflow`, `NonFiniteState`, `OutOfOperationalRange`, `MotionLimitExceeded`, `InvalidMass`, `InvalidShape`, `UnsupportedRotatingCcdPair`, `CcdInitialOverlap`, `CcdPositionCorrection`, `CcdBudgetExceeded`, `IndeterminateGeometry`, and `UnsatisfiableJoint`. Discard staging; locally committed poses/velocities/contacts/sleep, the previous snapshot, and the command cursor remain unchanged. Mark `PhysicsWorld` faulted and set `LastFault`, then throw `PhysicsSimulationException` carrying that fault and the original cause where applicable; return no step output. `Integration` handles that exception through the encompassing-world fault policy in [Chapter 13](13-jobs-and-frame-pipeline.md); `Physics` does not depend on or mark an ECS `World`. Retain admitted commands for explicit recovery/replay; never automatically retry or skip one.

A later Pose Sync/`Late`/`Structural`/`Extract` fault can occur after a valid stage 4 local physics commit. In that case physics state/cursor and earlier ECS writes may already have changed: do not claim whole-world rollback or silently reverse only physics. `Integration` suppresses all external tick events/receipts/snapshots, faults the encompassing `World`, and restores or reconstructs its prior committed *whole-world checkpoint* before any further tick/save, including physics, ECS, bindings, pending records, and origin. The previous externally published snapshot may remain visible with a fault indication. Local event use at `Stage` 6 never authorizes external I/O; only stage 9 publishes the complete encompassing tick.

Synchronous enqueue validation throws argument/ownership errors per [03](03-shared-conventions-and-clocks.md) before copying/admission. A formerly valid queued handle invalidated before application yields staged `CommandRejected` with `StaleHandle`; unsupported disabled/body-type operation yields `UnsupportedBodyOperation`. These expected rejections do not fault physics; consume/store their results only on valid local commit, or retain them with the journal on a physics fault. `Integration` publishes their receipts externally only at stage 9. `Structural` content rejection rejects its idle-boundary batch without partial creation and is distinct from a solver fault.

Post-solve ordinary penetration/joint error above slop is reported in `PhysicsStepStatistics`, not itself a rollback criterion: iterative solving is approximate. Gross joint initialization > 0.5 m or > 0.5 rad, contradictory fully fixed degrees of freedom, or a required row with zero effective mass and unsatisfied error is `UnsatisfiableJoint`. A standalone caller can inspect the last locally committed physics snapshot while faulted; further simulation/mutation is refused. Recovery replaces the service with a validated checkpoint restore or fresh recreation, allocating a new process `OwnerId`; it never revives old handles by installing their old owner. An integrated world may recover only through the encompassing checkpoint policy, not by independently resuming its physics service.

## 5. Broad phase, filtering, islands, and sleeping

`One` dynamic AABB tree indexes all enabled colliders; a mesh leaf additionally owns its immutable static triangle BVH. Proxy bounds contain the union of start/proposed-end bounds plus `ContactMargin`, and angular sweep uses the COM-centered enclosing radius. Fat bounds add 0.1 m and `2 * h * abs(v)` per component. If actual/swept bounds leave operational range, fault; fat padding may exceed the range and is not itself physical geometry. Reinsert whenever swept bounds leave the stored fat bound.

Insertion minimizes added perimeter in 2D / surface area in 3D including ancestor growth, ties by lowest subtree leaf handle. Leaf insertion, removal, and rotations maintain parent links and exact child-union bounds. Apply an AVL-style height rotation whenever child heights differ by > 1; choose the lower resulting area then lower leaf key on ties. `Query` every enabled non-static or sensor collider against the tree each microstep, including zero-velocity/sleeping bodies; do not lose stationary pairs by relying only on a moved list. Canonicalize, sort, deduplicate, then validate exact swept bounds before narrow phase. Rebuild when height exceeds `2 * ceil(log2(max(2,leafCount))) + 8`, by median longest-centroid-axis split, ties by handle. Pair output must match brute force on eligible actual/swept AABBs.

Mesh BVH construction splits median centroids on the longest centroid extent, X then Y then Z on ties; <= 4 triangles per leaf. Traversal orders lower node index first and checks actual bounds after coarse filtering. Count every triangle narrow-phase test against the tick budget. Bounds are conservative; no size overflow or NaN can become a Miss.

`CollisionFilter` is `(ulong CategoryBits, ulong MaskBits, short GroupIndex)`; category must be nonzero. Equal nonzero groups override masks: positive always permits, negative never permits. Otherwise both `(A.CategoryBits & B.MaskBits) != 0` and `(B.CategoryBits & A.MaskBits) != 0` are required. Same-body collider pairs never interact. A joint with `CollideConnected = false` (default) suppresses all pairs for its body pair, including sensors; any such joint wins. Non-sensor pairs require at least one dynamic body. Sensor pairs require at least one non-static body; static-static observations require an explicit query.

Build islands from dynamic bodies linked by non-sensor contacts within `ContactMargin` and enabled joints; static/kinematic bodies are boundary nodes, never links merging all dynamics through one floor. Sorted body handles seed DFS; sorted pair and joint keys define row order. Version one solves an island single-threaded. Independent islands may execute in parallel but merge outputs by keys, not completion time.

Wake a whole connected dynamic island for explicit wake/impulse/force, teleport, velocity/geometry/mass/filter/enable/joint edits, removal of supporting contacts, moving kinematic contact, or new contact with an awake dynamic. Sleeping bodies retain proxies/contact episode state and can be hit/queried. Static-body teleport/replacement wakes affected islands. An unchanged sleeping island is not re-solved solely to accumulate numerical noise; its contacts are still checked after relevant world edits.

At outer commit, an island sleeps only if every dynamic permits sleep, all three velocity thresholds held for the complete tick, no motion command/moving kinematic/motor is active, and contact/joint residuals <= 2 * their slops for >= 0.5 seconds. Count complete consecutive quiet ticks and compare to `ceil(0.5 * TickRate)`, avoiding a floating-point 30-tick off-by-one. A violation resets the count. Sleep atomically sets velocities and force accumulators to zero, retaining poses/manifolds. `EnableSleep = false` prevents automatic/manual sleep. Manual Sleep refuses unsatisfied constraints/motion; it is not a way to freeze an unstable stack. `InitiallyAwake` = false requires zero initial velocities and valid constraints; static sleep flags are inapplicable. Rebase alone neither wakes nor sleeps.

## 6. Mass, integration, and contact solver

Mass calculation uses translated bounded local coordinates, scaled binary32 formulas and stable compensated sums; source degeneracy uses certified/exact predicates. Validate finite mass/inertia, positive definiteness and the declared residual/conditioning before admission; overflow/uncertainty rejects mass admission, never invents mass. Primitive intrinsic products are evaluated from copied validated radius/half-extents and actual admitted density using bounded scaled/exact arithmetic before final destination rounding. They never multiply rounded `ShapeMeasure` statistics or require `TryGetSingle` to succeed. Use the exact dyadic `pi32` coefficient with bits `0x40490FDB` shared by [23's versioned primitive formulas](23-custom-physics-shapes-queries-and-ccd.md#12-inert-primitive-validation-and-statistics), not a correctly rounded mathematical-pi promise. Circle `m = density * pi32 * r^2`, `I = m * r^2 / 2`; sphere `m = density * 4 * pi32 * r^3 / 3`, `I = 2 * m * r^2 / 5`. Here `m` in an intrinsic inertia formula denotes its complete unrounded mass expression, not a previously published rounded mass. A box of full sizes x, y[, z] uses area/volume mass and `I2D = m * (x^2 + y^2) / 12` or 3D diagonal `m * (y^2 + z^2,x^2 + z^2,x^2 + y^2) / 12`.

For a CCW polygon with `cross_i = cross(p_i,p_next)`: area `A = sum(cross_i) / 2`; centroid `sum((p_i + p_next) * cross_i) / (6 * A)`; origin polar inertia `density * sum(cross_i * (dot(p_i,p_i) + dot(p_i,p_next) + dot(p_next,p_next))) / 12`. Shift to COM by subtracting `m * dot(center,center)`.

For an outward oriented polyhedron, faces are clockwise when viewed from outside in the left-handed (LH) world; numerical `cross(firstEdge, secondEdge)` gives the outward normal. Triangulate each face in stable vertex order into signed tetrahedra `(0,a,b,c)`. Each has `V = dot(a,cross(b,c)) / 6`, first moment `V * (a + b + c) / 4`, second moment diagonal `Q_xx = V * (sum(a_x^2,b_x^2,c_x^2) + a_x * b_x + a_x * c_x + b_x * c_x) / 10`, and mixed `Q_xy = V * (2 * sum(a_x * a_y,b_x * b_y,c_x * c_y) + sum(i_x * j_y for i != j)) / 20`. Sum signed moments, multiply by density, use `I = trace(Q) * Identity - Q`, and shift to COM. Rotate collider inertias into body local space and apply parallel-axis `m * (dot(r,r) * Identity - outer(r,r))`. Reject invalid volume/SPD/ranges, not absolute-value a wrongly wound mesh. A Z-reflected right-handed (RH) input reverses its face loops once before cooking; positive volume/mass and converted outward normals must agree.

Dynamic microstep velocity is `v = (v + h * (gravity * gravityScale + force / m)) / (1 + h * linearDamping)` and `omega = (omega + h * IworldInverse * torque) / (1 + h * angularDamping)`. `RotationLocked` zeros angular velocity and inverse inertia; torque then has no effect. Version one deliberately omits 3D gyroscopic `omega cross (I * omega)` and therefore is not a torque-free asymmetric-top simulator. This explicit approximation requires implementation characterization and is excluded from conservation claims.

Integrate displacement `segmentSeconds * v` into the private high/low COM with [08](08-binary32-kernels-and-predicates.md)'s compensated update and checked exact cell carries. Apply positional correction through the same update; never repeatedly add to a published pose. Pair/anchor subtraction retains compensation until bounded nearby evaluation under [07](07-spatial-coordinates-and-indexing.md). In 2D keep principal angle in (-pi, pi] and checked long turns for joint unwrapping; publish principal angle. Reduce after every angular increment, never run trigonometry on an indefinitely growing angle. In 3D use `qNext = Quaternion.Multiply(deltaWorld,q)`, where `deltaWorld` is the exponential of world `omega * segmentSeconds`; normalize using the scaled binary32 kernel, enforce nonzero norm, and canonicalize sign W > 0 (W = 0 uses first nonzero X/Y/Z positive). Multiplication rotates by its right argument then left argument, consistent with `System.Numerics` active vector rotation; central processing unit (CPU) row-matrix composition remains 01. `Position` angular impulses use the same exponential map.

For point anchors `xA`/`xB` and normal n from A to B, `rA = xA - cA`, `rB = xB - cB`, relative point velocity `u = vB + omegaB cross rB - vA - omegaA cross rA`, `vn = dot(n,u)`. Row Jacobian is `J = (-n,-rA cross n, + n, + rB cross n)`; `K = invMassA + invMassB + dot(rA cross n,IAinv * (rA cross n)) + dot(rB cross n,IBinv * (rB cross n))`. 2D replaces cross products with signed scalars. Applying row impulse lambda subtracts from A and adds to B via `MInverse * JTranspose * lambda`.

These are numerical cross products per [03](03-shared-conventions-and-clocks.md), not a new negated cross helper. Force-at-point torque uses axial r cross force; angular velocity/torque/impulse/quaternion vectors convert from RH by -H3, while anchors/linear velocity/force/contact normals convert by H3. Inertia tensors convert as H3 * I * H3. Positive `omegaZ` at r = (1,0,0) gives point velocity +Y, and positive `omegaY` turns local +Z toward +X. Hinge axis signs and `TargetSpeed` use this same axial convention; arbitrary geometry-axis sign choice does not change it.

Mixed friction `mu = sqrt(muA * muB)`; restitution `e = max(eA,eB)`. Capture pre-warm-start closing speed at each new physical impact; restitution target is `-e * vnPre` only if `vnPre < -RestitutionThreshold` and s <= GeometryTolerance. Otherwise target 0 for physical touching/penetrating points. A separated ordinary candidate s > `GeometryTolerance` has target `-s/remainingSeconds`, allowing it to approach actual contact rather than stopping in midair at positive `LinearSlop`. It has no restitution or friction yet. CCD-eligible separated pairs have no speculative velocity row or warm start: their exact sweep reaches time of impact (TOI) before impact solving. No penetration velocity bias is added: position correction is separate.

Scalar normal solve: `newLambda = max(0,oldLambda + (target - vn) / K)`, apply the difference, not the whole accumulation. `Zero` K with zero required motion is redundant; zero K with required motion is fault/refusal according to joint/contact type rules, not division by epsilon. A normalized row's nonzero K must exceed 1e-12 in its reciprocal-mass units; otherwise `IndeterminateGeometry` reports the row.

For a two-point 2D manifold use a 2x2 normal block: `Kij = Ji * MInverse * JjTranspose`, `b = vn - target - K * oldLambda`. Enumerate active sets in order both, point 0, point 1, neither; solve `K_active * x = -b_active`, require active x >= -1e-10 N s and inactive `K * x + b >= -1e-9 m / s`, clamp tiny negative x to zero. If `det(K) <= 1e-8 * K00 * K11`, use the two scalar rows in feature order. If a well-conditioned finite block has no admissible active set after recentered recomputation, fault `IndeterminateGeometry` with K/b rather than silently retain stale impulses. For 3D up to four points use scalar normal rows in feature order; do not claim equivalence to a four-point LCP solve.

Friction follows the normal solve each iteration for physical touching points only. In 2D `t = (-n.Y,n.X)`, scalar accumulated tangent impulse clamps to `[-mu * normalImpulseAtPoint, + mu * normalImpulseAtPoint]`. In 3D choose the coordinate axis least aligned with n (X/Y/Z tie order), `t1 = normalize(cross(axis,n))`, `t2 = cross(n,t1)`. Build symmetric2x2 tangent K, solve for accumulated impulse vector, project its Euclidean length onto the disk radius `mu * normalImpulse`, then apply the difference. Tangent block determinant <= `1e-12 * K00 * K11` or nonpositive Cholesky pivot is singular: use zero tangent impulse and explicit `DegenerateFriction` diagnostic, never NaNs. No rolling/torsional friction, adhesion, anisotropy, or separate static/dynamic coefficient in version one.

Warm starts require identical shape revision, feature keys and patch, anchor displacement <= 0.01 m and `dot(oldNormal, newNormal) >= 0.95`. Reproject cached friction into the new basis, clamp to its new disk, and clamp normal >= 0. Clear on edits, teleport, incompatible features or restitution impact. Across fixed equal-h microsteps reuse resting impulse once. Within one CCD-split microstep, carry already applied accumulations and do not warm-start/rescale/reapply them at each boundary; new contact rows start at zero. Keep impact impulses separate from the next microstep's resting cache. The frozen configuration never changes h in-place.

`Position` iterations recompute anchors, normals/effective masses and geometry, not just stale depth. For contact separation s use `C = clamp(s + LinearSlop,-MaxCorrection,0)`, pseudo impulse `-PositionFraction * C / K`; apply to poses only. Never accumulate/warm-start position impulses or change real velocities. Recompute broad-phase candidates after corrections so a correction cannot create an unobserved overlap. Before applying a position row,13 validates its proposed motion for CCD-eligible pairs; a joint's corrective translation cannot bypass a thin wall between samples. Record maximum final penetration and joint linear/angular residuals; tolerance tests in [33](33-physics-acceptance-and-reference.md), not prose, establish qualified stability.

If any body update from a position row/block would exceed 0.05 m translation or0.05 rad rotation, uniformly scale that entire pseudo impulse until both bounds hold for both bodies. This is a declared solver relaxation cap, not a clamp of integrated velocity motion. Sum the resulting actual increments for the microstep motion limit. Numerical warnings coalesce by (code, body/pair/joint) per tick with occurrence counts; retain <= 65536 diagnostic records within the storage cap and publish only after commit.

## 7. Joint set and equations

Joint endpoints are distinct bodies in this dimensional world, with at least one enabled dynamic endpoint. Disable either endpoint suspends the joint; removing an endpoint destroys it. Anchors are body-origin local coordinates, transformed using current body pose, not COM-local coordinates. `CollideConnected = false` by default. `Structural` creation validates frames and initial errors. Joint edits replace definitions, clear accumulated impulses, and wake connected islands.

| Joint | Typed data / constrained degrees of freedom |
|---|---|
| `Distance` 2D/3D | `LocalAnchorA`/B; Mode `FixedLength` or Range; finite `Length` or `MinLength`/`MaxLength` in `[0.05, 128]` m; optional `FrequencyHz` default 0 and finite `DampingRatio` in `[0, 2]`, default 1, for `FixedLength` only |
| Hinge2D | `LocalAnchorA`/B; `ReferenceAngle`; optional angular lower/upper limit; angular motor `TargetSpeed` and `MaxTorque`; constrains anchor XY, permits relative +Z rotation |
| Ball3D | `LocalAnchorA`/B; constrains anchor XYZ; no angular limits/motors |
| Hinge3D | `LocalAnchorA`/B; `LocalAxisA`/B unit length; `LocalReferenceA`/B unit perpendicular vectors; limits/motor as hinge2D; constrains anchor XYZ and two swing angles |
| Fixed 2D/3D | `LocalAnchorA`/B; `ReferenceAngle` / `RelativeRotation`; constrains translation and all relative rotation |

No slider, cone-twist, gear, pulley, rope/cloth, ragdoll generator, articulation/tree solver, or automatic break thresholds in version one. Compose supported joints explicitly, subject to residuals; no promise that arbitrary redundant loops are exact. `MaxTorque` is finite in `[0, 1e9]` N m, `TargetSpeed` within angular speed bound; limits are finite with lower <= upper and range width <= 2 * pi. A disabled motor has zero accumulation.

Every bilateral scalar row uses `delta = -(Jv + b + gamma * lambda) / (K + gamma)` and accumulated unrestricted lambda; apply delta. Hard rows have gamma = b = 0 in velocity solve; geometric drift is removed by separate position rows. Vector anchor constraints are solved as 2x2 (2D) / 3x3 (3D) SPD blocks with Cholesky pivots > 1e-12 of maximum diagonal; eliminate exactly locked redundant rows only when their error and requested velocity are within slop, otherwise `UnsatisfiableJoint`. Fixed angular rows are one scalar in 2D and a world XYZ 3x3 block in 3D.

Joint soft coefficients and motor impulse budget use the fixed microstep h, even through TOI splitting; retain their accumulated impulses throughout that microstep, so a motor cannot spend `MaxTorque` * h again per impact. Recompute geometry/J/K/b at each boundary without reapplying the stored impulse. Speculative limit targets use the remaining segment duration. This is a semi-implicit fixed-microstep approximation, not an exact spring integration over variable TOI intervals.

`Distance` uses `n = (xB - xA) / length` and the contact Jacobian. At length < `GeometryTolerance` use the stored last valid n; if none, use transformed local +X and emit `DegenerateJointAxis`; hard `FixedLength` then corrects from that axis, not NaN. Soft distance: `mEff = 1 / K`, `omega0 = 2 * pi * FrequencyHz`, `k = mEff * omega0^2`, `d = 2 * mEff * DampingRatio * omega0`, `gamma = 1 / (h * (d + h * k))`, `b = (length - Length) * h * k * gamma`. Require `0<FrequencyHz<=0.25/h`; `FrequencyHz` = 0 selects hard row, never evaluates the division. Soft rows have no position correction.

Range lower row uses C = length-`MinLength`, lambda >= 0, target `-max(C,0)/remainingSeconds`; upper row C = length-`MaxLength`, lambda <= 0, target `-min(C,0)/remainingSeconds`. Activate only at a bound within slop or if predicted Jv would cross it; remove stale accumulation on becoming inactive. Equal lower/upper becomes one hard bilateral distance row. `Position` solve corrects only violated bounds; do not pull a free interior length toward a hidden rest length.

Point-anchor blocks have linear Jacobians along world coordinate axes and their lever-arm angular terms. For every hard scalar positional error C use `sign(C) * min(max(abs(C) - Slop,0),MaxCorrection)`, then impulse `-0.2 * Ccorrected / K`; Slop is linear/angular as appropriate. Anchor/angular vectors reduce their magnitude by the corresponding slop before clamping and block solving. Fixed angle error is shortest `angleB-angleA-ReferenceAngle`; 3D target is `qTarget = qA * RelativeRotation`, error is shortest axis-angle of `qB * conjugate(qTarget)`, expressed in world axes. Limit angular pose updates to 0.05 rad per iteration. Reference quaternions are unit; sign is not a distinct configuration.

Hinge3D axes `aA = RA * LocalAxisA`, `aB = RB * LocalAxisB` must start within 0.5 rad; swing angle is `atan2(length(cross(aA,aB)),dot(aA,aB))`. Its error vector is normalized cross times this angle, or zero for aligned axes, resolved along two deterministic tangents to `aA`. The angular rows use `J = (-t, + t)`; antiparallel axes after solving are `IndeterminateGeometry`, not an arbitrary flip. Rotate local reference vectors to world, project/normalize onto the plane perpendicular to `aA`, and compute twist `atan2(dot(aA,cross(refA,refB)),dot(refA,refB))`; unwrap continuously using previous principal twist and checked turns. Projection norm below `AxisTolerance` faults. Hinge2D uses unwrapped `angleB-angleA-ReferenceAngle`. The 3D reference vectors define zero twist; no unspecified extra reference rotation is inferred.

Hinge motor uses J = (-axis, + axis), b = -`TargetSpeed`, gamma = 0, clamps total impulse to +/-`MaxTorque` * h. A limit row takes precedence; disable motor action toward an active violated bound, allow action away. Lower/upper angular limits use the same signed inequalities as range distance, replacing meters with radians; equal limits become bilateral. Hard position solve corrects only violated angular bounds. Reaction forces/torques are final accumulated row impulses divided by h, reported with duration; they are diagnostics, not a hidden auto-break feature.

Ordering applies within each selected solve/structural batch, not across execution barriers. Out-of-window admission is argument misuse before copying; byte/count/storage caps include every retained batch/key.

Nonstructural variants are `ApplyForce`, `ApplyForceAtPoint`, `ApplyTorque`, `ApplyImpulse`, `ApplyImpulseAtPoint`, `SetVelocity`, `SetKinematicTarget`, `Teleport`, `SetEnabled`, `SetFilter`, Wake, Sleep. `Structural` variants are `CreateBody(definition plus copied attachments)`, `AddCollider`, `RemoveCollider`, `ReplaceCollider`, `ReplaceMass`, `ReplaceBodyType`, `CreateJoint`, `ReplaceJoint`, `DestroyJoint`, `DestroyBody`. `DestroyBody` defaults to `BodyDestroyed` and accepts explicit Unbound from an adapter/application; it does not inspect entities. Replacement operations explicitly choose Preserve/Clear velocity; static/kinematic replacement clears. `Structural` results use named sealed payload classes derived from the engine-owned `PhysicsStructuralResult2D`/3D private-protected base, not C# closed/union declarations.

Creation dependencies within an idle structural batch use dimensional named `readonly` `BodyReference2D/3D` values on command endpoints. Kind enum is Invalid(default)/Existing/`Pending`; validated factories accept a `BodyHandle` or `PhysicsRequestId`, and the corresponding named accessor rejects the wrong kind. This is a small domain reference, not a language union. `CreateBody` attachments need no existing `BodyHandle`. Resolve references only after reservation; forward references are permitted in the same admission batch and invalid/cyclic dependencies reject the batch. Request IDs bind owner/admission tick/full key, never masquerade as body handles.

Public standalone `Step(long tick)` returns either `PhysicsStepOutput2D` or `PhysicsStepOutput3D`, the completed named domain output. An unexpected solver/capacity/numerical failure throws `PhysicsSimulationException` with a non-`null` `PhysicsFault` after section 4's staging rollback and `Faulted`/`LastFault` update. The exception derives from `InvalidOperationException` and retains the original cause when present. There is no `PhysicsStepResult` success/error union, arbitrary-delta API, or successful default output. `PhysicsStepOutput` contains `Tick`, `FrameRevision`, `StructuralRevision`, immutable `BodyStates`/raw events/`CommandResults`, and `Statistics`. `NextTick`/committed state/events do not advance on the exception. `Step` invokes no arbitrary callback; returning a complete output and exposing it through `ReadEvents` constitutes immediate standalone local publication. `Integration` calls the same `Step` at stage 4, retains success tick-locally until stage 9, and handles the named exception through the world-fault policy in [Chapter 13](13-jobs-and-frame-pipeline.md) without inventing rollback of other writes. A public encompassing `World.StepTicks` remains an optional `Hosting`/`Integration` adapter, not a `Physics` dependency.

Dimensional `PrepareStructural(long admissionTick)` and `PrepareStructural(long admissionTick, PhysicsRebaseRequest2D rebase)`, or the matching `PhysicsRebaseRequest3D` overload, validate/sort that batch and reserves all physics storage/handles with no live mutation; it returns either `PhysicsStructuralPreparation2D` or `PhysicsStructuralPreparation3D`. This sealed immutable domain response has a `Status` enum (`Prepared`/`Rejected`), nullable `Plan`, and nullable `Rejection(PhysicsFault)`: exactly the applicable payload is non-`null`, and construction is engine-controlled. Expected content/version/budget rejection uses that response; misuse or unexpected allocation/invariant failure throws. `CommitStructural(plan)` accepts the prepared plan and returns either `PhysicsStructuralReceipt2D` or `PhysicsStructuralReceipt3D` after checking owner/base revisions and swapping the prepared state. The receipt contains immutable typed results, resulting `FrameRevision`/`StructuralRevision`, and an optional dimensional `PhysicsRebaseReceipt2D`/`PhysicsRebaseReceipt3D`. It requires an idle owner boundary with no active queries/jobs/`Step`. The plan holds the exclusive operation gate, pins immutable assets, is single-use/disposable, and abandonment frees reservations without consuming commands. `Commit` allocates nothing and invokes no callback. `Integration` preflights ECS/binding reservations before committing that physics plan; standalone callers need no ECS reservation. When a boundary combines structural commands and rebase, the optional request produces one prepared/committed plan: validate/apply canonical spatial commands, then publish the derived frame change in section 9 to the resulting graph, including new-body caches and retained pending spatial witnesses. A rejection changes neither graph nor frame and consumes neither batch; successful commit increments `StructuralRevision` once and increments `FrameRevision` once only when a frame change is included. Never prepare competing structural/rebase plans for the same service or commit one merely to discover whether the other can succeed.

`Close() -> PhysicsClosure2D or PhysicsClosure3D` runs at an idle owner boundary in `Ready` or faulted state, joins private work, and returns a copied immutable record containing `LocalTick`, `HasCompletedStep`, `FrameRevision`, and raw events. It moves pending ends to the closure boundary while preserving their observed tick/reason/witnesses, appends `WorldDisposed` ends for remaining episodes, and sorts by section 3's key with `PublicationTick = NextTick` and `TickFraction = 1`. `Step`/structural preflight reserves terminal-output capacity for the resulting live/pending episode set; `Close` builds and validates that output before releasing storage, invokes no callback, then sets `Status = Disposed`. Repeated `Close` returns the same closure; `IDisposable.Dispose` delegates to `Close`, and nullable `LastClosure` remains readable after disposal. Previously copied outputs survive. `Integration` uses its stage 9 episode ledger for external closure rather than blindly forwarding this local batch.

`Query`/event/result callbacks are synchronous, `scoped`, cannot await or mutate/recursively step/query the service, but may copy values/enqueue future commands. They run only when explicitly invoked outside solving. Exceptions propagate to the caller or `Integration`'s callback-fault policy and never pretend to roll back a valid local solve. A tick-local callback exception before chapter [13](13-jobs-and-frame-pipeline.md)'s stage 9 commit marker suppresses that tick's external publication and requires whole-world checkpoint recovery. An external subscriber exception after the marker follows chapter [13](13-jobs-and-frame-pipeline.md)'s isolated delivery-failure policy: the tick/ledger remain committed and other subscribers continue.

## 9. Standalone rebase and integrated structural admission

Each service owns a dimensional `SpatialFrame2`/`SpatialFrame3` and `FrameRevision`; no scene/global-origin lookup is required. The single public boundary is `PrepareStructural`/`CommitStructural`, with overloads accepting no rebase or a dimensional `PhysicsRebaseRequest2D`/`PhysicsRebaseRequest3D`. A rebase-only boundary uses that same API with an empty structural batch, matching preparation/plan/receipt types and the admitted idle tick. There are no separate public rebase methods. These owner-thread operations exclude active queries/jobs/steps/competing plans. Preparation has `Prepared`/`Rejected` status and exactly one plan/rejection payload. Integration runs it at stage 7; standalone callers select an idle boundary.

Preflight requires matching `ExpectedFrameRevision`, canonical/profile-compatible `NewFrameOrigin`, checked next revisions and reserved replacement-cache storage within 512 MiB. Validate every cache that actually uses the changed frame; a distant occupied cell need not fit a camera's frame because its own calculation neighborhood remains spatially indexed. COMs/targets/history/world witnesses retain spatial identity. Pair/island frames remain bounded and residency-pinned. Mismatched revision yields `FrameRevisionMismatch`; invalid arguments throw, while expected range/capacity refusal returns a named rejection before mutation.

The plan owns prepared derived caches and records owner/base structural/frame revisions, old/new dimensional frames and local tick. It pins dependencies, is single-use/disposable and holds the idle gate. Abandonment releases reservations only. Commit validates owner/use/revisions before swapping caches/frame, increments frame/structural revisions once, and returns named old/new-frame/version/tick properties. It allocates nothing and calls no subscriber.

Persistent cell/high/low positions, targets, historical spatial poses and spatial contact witnesses do not shift. Local shape vertices/contact/joint anchors, normals/rotations, velocities/forces/torques, mass, feature IDs/impulses, collider revisions, episodes and sleep counters remain unchanged. Frame-only rebasing emits no contact/sleep transitions and changes neither conservative candidate membership nor proxy identity. A carry during actual integration is distinct from a frame change.

Returned immutable outputs retain their original frame descriptor and spatial witnesses. Integration may re-evaluate only frame-relative copied observations for stage 9; it does not rerun Late systems. Canonical spatial targets remain meaningful across frame changes. Explicitly frame-relative queued inputs retain their declared revision and reject when stale; they are never reinterpreted as current-frame coordinates.

Integration prepares all participating frame/cache updates before any stage 7 commit. Rejection leaves their current frames unchanged. A later fault after valid commits follows [13](13-jobs-and-frame-pipeline.md)'s checkpoint recovery, not imaginary cross-service rollback. Standalone commit immediately exposes its coherent local frame/receipt with no ECS dependency.

## 10. Persistent checkpoints and StrictLocal continuation

[27](27-state-serialization-replay-and-recovery.md) owns save/replay policy, codecs and encompassing-world activation. `Physics` capture/restore remains `Core`/BCL-only, including standalone use; it does not inspect ECS layout or reference `TerraFX.Serialization`. Integrated saves capture physics only from the successful stage 9 whole-world checkpoint, never the newer local output of a failed encompassing tick. Standalone capture selects its `Ready`, locally committed state at an idle boundary outside queries/jobs/`Step`/prepared plans.

Public `CaptureCheckpoint(PhysicsCheckpointMode mode, PhysicsIdentityCatalog2D or PhysicsIdentityCatalog3D identities) -> PhysicsCheckpoint2D or PhysicsCheckpoint3D` copies immutable state; mode is `Standard` or `StrictLocal`. The caller/codec supplies a frozen catalog containing `WorldGuid` and unique nonempty stable globally unique identifiers (GUIDs) for all referenced body/collider/shape/joint identities, retained historical identities, and pending creations. Catalog entries pair each GUID with its typed runtime handle or request ID only in memory. `BindingId` remains separate opaque data and is not a unique persistent object ID: `Guid.Empty` and duplicate bindings are legal. Missing/duplicate catalog entries reject before producing a checkpoint; capture neither changes simulation state nor consumes commands.

The checkpoint's logical schema contains `FormatVersion`, Mode, `WorldGuid`, `Core`/`Physics` build digests, runtime/operating system (OS)/instruction set architecture (ISA) and numerical/layout profile identities, frozen options, `NextTick`/`HasCompletedStep`, dimensional spatial frame/settings/`FrameRevision`/`StructuralRevision` and the state below. Standalone `StrictLocal` validates this physics profile; integrated restore additionally validates chapter [27](27-state-serialization-replay-and-recovery.md)'s full engine/module/content profile. The value owns its copied backing storage; no pointer, pool lease, unrestricted `ref` or caller-mutable array escapes. Codecs emit canonical fields per [27](27-state-serialization-replay-and-recovery.md), not raw object/struct memory. All object relationships use catalog GUIDs, including shapes, endpoints, pair members, commands and historical event witnesses; pending references use pending-creation GUIDs plus their original admission tick/key. No process `OwnerId`, thread ID, raw handle tuple, native pointer, pool address, padding or profiling/wall-time timestamp is persisted.

| `Physics` state group | Required restore state |
|---|---|
| Numeric/body state | Canonical int32 cell/compensated binary32 COM/origin, binary32 rotation/velocities, turns, mass/inertia and frozen body fields; force/torque accumulators, target/progress, enabled/filter/material state, revisions and both previous/current interpolation snapshots; preserve already stored public `float` bits too |
| Shapes/colliders/joints | Stable GUID/content revisions and cooked geometry with exact vertex/edge/face/subshape order and feature IDs; attachments, local frames/anchors, joint limits/motors/soft state, accumulated impulses and continuous-angle history |
| Contacts/sleep | Ordered pair/patch/feature caches, local/world witnesses, normals/tangents and warm-start impulses/durations; active episodes/sequences, island sleep/quiet counters and wake state, without recomputing contacts through simulation |
| Commands/events | `Pending` command payloads with original `Stage`/`SystemOrder`/Partition/Sequence and admission/frame revisions, retained duplicate-key sets, consumption/result/event cursors, ordered pending ends and their historical GUID/slot witnesses; last committed raw event/command/structural-result batches and logical statistics, excluding profiling time |
| `StrictLocal` arena layout | For bodies/colliders/shapes/joints: slot `Index`/`Generation`, live/free/retired status and occupant GUID, next unused index, creation/traversal ordinals and their next values where used, exact free-list and dense traversal/allocation order; preserve holes and retirement, not merely live counts |
| `StrictLocal` traversal topology | Proxy fat/actual bounds and leaf associations, tree root/node indices/links/heights and node allocator/free-list order; persistent contact/joint storage order and any other order that affects enumeration, budgets, solver rows or later allocation |

Slot `Index`/`Generation` and tree indices above are logical layout metadata, not serialized cross-owner references. Tombstones additionally retain their historical slot/generation so a removed GUID does not resolve accidentally to a reused live occupant. Maps may be GUID-sorted for canonical encoding; the declared layout/traversal arrays and free lists remain in their saved order. `Standard` loads may repack/reallocate these layouts and rebuild acceleration structures, with remapped GUID references and no exact-continuation promise. `StrictLocal` must restore them, including collider ordering because it controls pair/event/row order; it cannot recreate through GUID-sorted `CreateBody`/`CookShape`/`CreateJoint` calls.

Static `PhysicsWorld2D.RestoreCheckpoint(PhysicsOwnerContext context, PhysicsCheckpoint2D)` returns a sealed immutable `PhysicsRestoreResponse2D`; `PhysicsWorld3D.RestoreCheckpoint(PhysicsOwnerContext context, PhysicsCheckpoint3D)` returns a sealed immutable `PhysicsRestoreResponse3D`. Each response has `Status` `Restored`/`Rejected`. A `Restored` response has `FreshWorld` and immutable `GuidToHandle`/`GuidToRequest` maps; a `Rejected` response has `PhysicsFault` and no world/maps. Each method validates the decoded schema, identities, references, limits, canonical numeric state, and declared profile, reserves all storage, and constructs an unpublished fresh service with its internally allocated `OwnerId`. `StrictLocal` fills the saved slots/generations/order/topology under that new owner; all live handles, request IDs, caches, and historical raw payloads are remapped, while old source handles remain foreign. Expected decoded-content/profile rejection exposes no partial service and leaves the old service/world active; validation failure is `InvalidCheckpoint`, and incompatible strict numerical/layout/profile state is `ReplayProfileMismatch`. Misuse/unexpected allocation/invariant failure throws conventionally after staged cleanup. `Integration` performs the whole-world validation/`Begin` activation defined in [Chapter 27](27-state-serialization-replay-and-recovery.md) and owns ECS/binding remapping.

The response maps expose `IReadOnlyDictionary<Guid, BodyHandle2D>`/`IReadOnlyDictionary<Guid, BodyHandle3D>` and `IReadOnlyDictionary<Guid, PhysicsRequestId>` as declared in [30](30-public-api-review-surface.md#8-standalone-physics). Their contents are immutable response snapshots, not live views of the new world's mutable stores; private backing can be frozen, immutable or custom under [02's boundary policy](02-dotnet-api-memory-and-ownership.md#public-type-boundary). No writable alias escapes. Map representation/enumeration does not change the checkpoint's canonical encoding or restored traversal order; callers cannot depend on a concrete dictionary type or its enumeration order. The existing `Restored`/`Rejected` null applicability, remapped identity and ownership rules remain unchanged.

Restore validates already-canonical finite binary32/compensated/quaternion bits and then copies them unchanged, including permitted signed zero. Validate quaternion norm/sign under the saved field's numeric profile; reject noncanonical state instead of normalizing it. Do not rerun authoring pose normalization, mass calculation, shape quantization/recooking, sign canonicalization or warm-up `Step`. Derived transforms/inverse caches may be rebuilt only by the declared profile without changing canonical fields, feature ordering or observable traversal/budget behavior; otherwise preserve their logical state or reject `StrictLocal`. Restore retains `NextTick`, impulses, quiet counters, interpolation history and pending cursors exactly and emits no `Begin`/End/Sleep/Wake on activation. Restored last batches remain readable with remapped identities, not automatically re-published as new transitions.

`StrictLocal` integrated continuation also requires chapter [27](27-state-serialization-replay-and-recovery.md)'s exact ECS archetype/chunk ordinals, row GUID order, next ordinals, slot generations/retirement and free lists. That is `Integration`/ECS's obligation, not state owned by `Physics`: restoring physics correctly while repacking entity rows can still change command partitions and solver inputs. Canonical checksums include the saved order/allocator metadata and GUID-based state, excluding new `OwnerIds` and cosmetic timings; authoritative application logic cannot derive persistent behavior from an `OwnerId`. Future persistent GUID assignments must be recorded input or come from restored authoritative identity-generation state, not newly sampled randomness during replay. Exact continuation is qualified only for chapter [27](27-state-serialization-replay-and-recovery.md)'s identical engine/content/runtime/OS/ISA/numerical profile, never inferred from fixed stepping or advertised cross-platform.
